Drone Hunter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Drone Hunter does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

No data leaves your device

The app has no analytics, no accounts, no advertising, and makes no network requests to any server.

Camera

The game uses the rear camera for two things: position tracking (so stepping aside dodges a drone) and the optional Camera AR view. Camera frames are processed on the device in real time and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Local multiplayer

In two-player mode, the app connects directly to one nearby phone over Apple's MultipeerConnectivity (Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi). Only game state — positions, shots, scores — is exchanged, directly between the two devices. Nothing is sent over the internet and nothing is stored afterwards.

On-device storage

Your high score is saved on the phone. Sound effects are generated by the app and cached on the phone. Both are removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
